# Yuzuu Studio

Yuzuu Studio is a premium web agency focused primarily on tailor-made websites.

## Primary Service

The primary service is the design and development of premium custom websites for clients.

Client websites are intended to include basic SEO and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) as part of the standard offering.

## Business Model

The Yuzuu Studio website serves as both:

- A portfolio
- A lead-generation and client acquisition system

The intended client journey is:

1. Search or discovery
2. Visit Yuzuu Studio website
3. Explore portfolio
4. Understand quality and positioning
5. Schedule a call
6. Discovery
7. Proposal
8. Custom website project

## Discoverability

Yuzuu Studio's own website has high priority for SEO and GEO.

The objective is to maximize discoverability and entity clarity across traditional search engines and AI-powered discovery systems, including Google, Bing, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity.

There is no guarantee of ranking or inclusion in any search or AI system.

## Additional Offering

Yuzuu Studio also develops and offers MerchantOne, a separate business-system product that may include:

- Inventory management
- Billing
- POS
- Business website
- Optional e-commerce
- Future business functionality

MerchantOne is an additional offering and is not necessarily included in every client website project.

## Current State

The Yuzuu Studio domain has not yet been acquired.

The knowledgebase is being developed as a local-first system to serve as the canonical source of truth for the studio's business knowledge, decisions, projects, research, and operational state.
